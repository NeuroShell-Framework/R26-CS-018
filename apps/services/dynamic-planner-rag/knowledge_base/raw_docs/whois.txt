WHOIS

Purpose: passive reconnaissance. Queries public WHOIS registries for
ownership, contact, and allocation data. No packets are sent to the target.

Syntax: whois <target>

The target is the only argument. No options or flags are needed.

Example: whois <target>

Notes:
  - Read-only, safe, and stealthy.
  - Output includes registrar, netrange, CIDR, and abuse contact.
  - Use for scope validation and asset ownership checks.
  - The whois command takes exactly one argument: the target.
  - Do not add servers, flags or options.
